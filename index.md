## whoami

C++ developer, eight-plus years deep in video — VMS, video analytics,
CCTV. The full stack of pixels-to-pipeline: RTSP/ONVIF ingest,
OpenCV / FFmpeg processing, OpenGL rendering. The kind of code where
memory matters and a dropped frame becomes a customer call. Long
enough at the language to have cycled through several phases of
loving it and being annoyed at it — which is, I think, the right
relationship to have.

Originally trained as a physicist. Back then *lean and mean* still
applied to both halves — somewhere along the way programming took
over the brain, most of the maths quietly evaporated, and I'm now
mostly the second half. Briefly taught programming to undergrads
while finishing the degree.

Read and speak English comfortably; somewhere around C1 on a good
day. Once collected a junior financial-advisor certification from
[NAUFOR](https://naufor.ru/), which turned out useful mostly for
not zoning out when a project drifts into the finance domain.

Off-keyboard: PADI Open Water diver, plus a small soldering corner
for hobby electronics — the kind of circuits that produce things
nobody asked for. The iron occasionally wins.

---

Currently employed full-time and not actively searching. A genuinely
interesting long-term C++ role — backend, video streaming,
performance-critical work, with room to actually make technical
calls — will still get my attention. A good permanent gig beats any
one-off project, however clever the project sounds, so freelance
side work is a higher bar these days than it used to be.
